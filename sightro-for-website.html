THE EXPERIENCE SUITE

Four ways to help buyers experience the property.

Each tool answers a different buyer question. Together, they create a richer decision journey—and more useful signals.

DreamRoom

Can I see myself in this home?

AI-powered room visualization based on natural-language prompts.

LifeEcho

Can I see my life here?

Explore personal scenarios around family, work, hobbies and everyday life.

NeighborhoodTour

What is life like beyond the front door?

Explore nearby schools, parks, cafés, hospitals and everyday amenities.

LocalWatch

What is changing around this property?

Surface meaningful upcoming developments, infrastructure and local news.

THE PAYOFF

The experience creates the intelligence.

Sightro captures meaningful behavioral signals after a buyer engages. Your sales team sees more than a name and an email—they see context.
