SIGHTRO MISSION

Sightro is on a mission to turn property browsing into clear buyer intent.

Times have changed, and so has how people search for homes.

Today’s buyers want to explore on their own terms. They do not just look at floor plans—they want to understand the lifestyle, the neighborhood, and the day-to-day experience of living there.

Yet on most real estate websites, that browsing remains completely anonymous. Buyers click through photos, look around, and leave without sharing what they actually care about. Meanwhile, sales teams are left guessing.

Sightro adds intelligent, interactive experiences to real estate websites. We help buyers explore properties, lifestyles, and locations naturally—while capturing richer signals in the background. Instead of basic form fills, we turn everyday browsing into actionable buyer preferences delivered straight to your sales team.

We are removing the guesswork from property marketing, starting with our priority markets across the United States and the UAE.

Our Story & Founder

Built by Alive Mind, Inc. Focused on how people actually buy.

Our story began with a simple disconnect in the real estate market.

Teams were investing heavily to drive traffic to their websites, but visitors were treated to static pages and passive image galleries. Sales teams were handed cold leads with zero context not knowing what features, view, or neighborhood perks actually sparked the buyer’s interest.

We created Sightro, a product of Alive Mind, Inc., to bridge that gap. We did not build it to chase tech trends; we built an AI-powered experience that gives buyers an interactive way to discover a property, while giving sales teams the exact insights they need to have meaningful conversations.

Today, Sightro helps property businesses in the US, the UAE, and global markets turn website curiosity into qualified, motivated buyers.
