THE PROBLEM

Your website shows the property. It does not tell you who is interested — or why.

Waiting for a form fill means missing out on highly engaged prospects. See exactly who is looking, what they are looking at, and when to reach out to close the deal.

THE SIGHTRO EXPERIENCE

Give buyers more reasons to explore.

Four interactive experiences turn a passive property website into an active discovery journey.

DreamRoom

See the home differently.

Let buyers reimagine rooms with AI and explore what the property could become.

LifeEcho

See life here before moving in.

Simulate everyday scenarios around the home, lifestyle and priorities that matter to the buyer.

NeighborhoodTour

Explore beyond the front door.

Understand the surrounding area through places, amenities and the everyday destinations buyers care about.

LocalWatch

See what is coming next.

AI surfaces meaningful local developments — infrastructure, new amenities and community investment — in concise, useful updates.

THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER

Every interaction creates a clearer picture of buyer intent.

Sightro tracks engagement time, features used, and properties viewed, automatically rating the lead for your sales team to act on immediately.
